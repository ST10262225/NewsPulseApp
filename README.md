How to use our news app called NewsPulse.

This is the link to the demontration video: https://youtu.be/LWaoQfJ_xDA?si=UPl-Q-UQklycyOQr 

1. When you open it you will land on the launcher page which is the login page.
2. if you dont have an account, you will click on the "register here" under the login button then it will take you to the register page.
3. Fill in your information and make sure you remember your password. When you have registered succefully then it will re-direct you to the login page.
4. We have an SSO(single sing-on), you can sign in using your email address.
5. Then you log in, it will take you to the homepage where the current news will appear then the user can become informed.
6. The news are supplied by the local API and its distributing local news (SA) only.
7. On the bottom navigation bar there's downloads, weather, refresh and settings butttons.
8. When the user wants updates on the weather they can just click on the "sun" icon, then you will land on the weather page where the weather API supplies current weather forecast.
9. In this app the user can click on settings and set their settings to what they want. The user can turn from light mode to dark mode, they can switch on the notifications.
10. The Downloads button you can click it then it will show you all the articles you have downloaded, then these articles you can view them offline, its offline sync.
11. You can also logout on the settings page.
12. You can share the articles by clicking the share button at the bottom of the article on your left you can share to whatsapp, messages email etc.
13. The app has the search bar at the top where you can search for the article you want.
