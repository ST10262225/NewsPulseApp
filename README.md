How to use our news app called NewsPulse.

This is the link to the demontration video: https://youtu.be/KS1-BVy9azk 

1. When you open it you will land on the launcher page which is the login page.
2. if you dont have an account, you will click on the "register here" under the login button then it will take you to the register page.
3. Fill in your information and make sure you remember your password. When you have registered succefully then it will re-direct you to the login page.
4. Then you log in, loit will take you to the homepage where the current news will appear then the user can become informed.
5. On the bottom navigation bar theres, sports, weather and settings butttons.
6. When the user wants updates on the weather they can just click on the "sun" icon, then you will land on the weather page where the weather API supplies current weather forecast.
7. In this app the user can click on settings and set their settings to what they want. The user can turn from light mode to dark mode, they can switch on the notifications.
8. You can also logout on the settings page.
